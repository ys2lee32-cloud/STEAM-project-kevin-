Result

It was very successful since our hypothesis and logic were correct.

Muddy, soft, flexible, and not smooth balls like rubber (slime) and clay had more friction. This caused them to take more time to roll down the ramp.

Hard, smooth, and less flexible balls like wood, urethane, and metal had less friction, making them roll down the ramp faster.

We proved this to be true after analyzing the results of the experiment.
