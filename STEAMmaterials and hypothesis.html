Hypothesis

If the surface or texture of the ball is smoother and harder, then the ball will roll faster down the ramp because it will have less friction with the ramp. It will also have less friction since it does not stick closely to the ramp.

Rougher surfaces or softer, squishier, and muddier materials will roll down more slowly because they create more friction when rolling on the wooden ramp. These materials stick more closely to the surface, which reduces speed.

By using Newton’s Laws of Motion, we can predict that different amounts of friction caused by different surfaces will change the speed and time of the ball. Mass and gravity stay constant, so friction is the main factor affecting motion.

We predict that the urethane or metal ball will be the fastest, while the clay or rubber (slime ball) will be the slowest.
